\subsection{Testing Strategy}
Testing is layered (unit, integration, functional, non-functional, boundary, concurrency and live devices); the simulated transport makes routing and reliability tests deterministic, so a failure reproduces from a seed. The \NTests\ test cases were run completely \NRuns\ times. \input{generated/tests_summary.tex}

\subsection{Unit Testing}
Each module is tested alone: the packet format (47 tests: round trips, field ranges, truncation of each region, bad magic, signable bytes), the cost function and graph, Dijkstra with caching and \texttt{RESCUE\_\_}, gossip, discovery and link quality, the dedup set, priority queue, ACK tracker and store-and-forward bounds, AES-GCM and Ed25519, the SQLite log, the relay and the simulated transport.

\subsection{Integration Testing}
\texttt{test\_node} (27 tests) covers discovery and relay on one transport, multi-hop origination, ACK, the message lifecycle, end-to-end encryption and signing, retry exhaustion, eviction and expiry; \texttt{test\_ack\_multihop} checks that ACKs and retries cross relays correctly; \texttt{test\_api} drives front end, back end and engine through FastAPI and its WebSocket; \texttt{test\_lan\_transport} runs a three-node mesh over real TCP. The screens were checked by running them (Section~\ref{sec:shots}).

\subsection{Non-Functional Testing}
\label{sec:nonfunc}
\textbf{Reliability:} delivery at 0--50\,\% loss (Figure~\ref{fig:results}a). \textbf{Scalability:} 10 to 65 nodes (Table~\ref{tab:scale}). \textbf{Performance:} latency by priority (Section~\ref{sec:perfresults}), throughput and live latency (Table~\ref{tab:live}). \textbf{Availability:} recovery over 50 simulated trials and 8 live failovers. \textbf{Security:} a relay without the key never sees plaintext; tampered ciphertext, a wrong key, a forged signature and an unknown signer are rejected; signatures verify across relays. \textbf{Usability:} one 1.2\,s hold on one button with plain-language status; no timed study with participants (Section~\ref{sec:limits}).

\subsection{Boundary and Invalid-Input Testing}
\label{sec:boundary}
Tests cover empty, header-only and truncated input; a bad magic value; out-of-range TTL and lengths; over-long paths and node ids; wrong-length signatures and key sizes; ciphertext shorter than the nonce; and a send to an unknown destination (held, not an exception). \texttt{test\_robustness} feeds 3\,000 random and 1\,000 bit-flipped packets to the parser and 200 garbage buffers to a running node, which must only reject them with \texttt{PacketError} and keep working.

\subsection{Concurrency and Stress Testing}
\label{sec:stress}
200 simultaneous sends over two hops arrived exactly once with every ACK returned; 50 SOS and 50 routine messages from two senders all arrived intact; a packet replayed five times was delivered once. Throughput to 2\,000 sends and the congested priority scenario complete the stress tests; with all shared state on one thread, races and deadlocks cannot occur.

\subsection{Limitations and Untested Scenarios}
\label{sec:limits}
\textbf{No radio hardware:} interference, fading and mobility were not tested. \textbf{Live timing is loopback;} the two-laptop run was not timed. \textbf{No usability study with participants.} The browser checks are scripts run by hand, not part of the automated suite. \textbf{Scale:} nothing above 65 nodes, no soak test. \textbf{Relays do not retain.} \textbf{Security:} one pre-shared key serves the mesh; no external review.
